Original baseline execution model & reproducibility

  1. Original EMA bias: previous completed daily close versus that same day's EMA20. EMA uses α = 2/21, seeded at the first daily close on 1 Jan 2015, with over a year of warm-up. Current-day daily data never influences today's trade.
  2. Original EMA filter: |previous close / previous EMA20 − 1| ≤ selected percentage. Equal close and EMA means no directional bias.
  3. Range: high and low of every bar from ORB start through ORB end, inclusive. Default 09:20–09:59 contains 40 bars.
  4. Breakout: original strategy uses the EMA bias and ORB close breakout. The PDH/PDL variant ignores EMA entirely: first eligible close strictly above both ORB high and previous-day high is LONG; strictly below both ORB low and previous-day low is SHORT. Wick-only crossings do not qualify. Fill is the breakout bar's close; a gap outside the range qualifies. Daily entries follow the selected strategy and Daily entries control.
  1. Risk: stop distance = selected multiplier × range. Target = selected R × initial risk. Levels use exact arithmetic without tick rounding.
  2. Exit: stop/target become active on the next candle after entry. A stop gap fills at the worse open; a favorable target gap fills only at the target. If both levels occur within a bar and neither is resolved by the open, stop wins. Levels have priority over force exit on 15:10.
  3. Metrics: R = signed index points / initial stop distance. Cost-adjusted R subtracts round-trip points / initial risk. R profit factor is gross positive R / absolute negative R. Points profit factor uses point P&L. Quantity = lot size × the applicable first/second entry lots, default 65 × 1. ₹ P&L and ₹ risk = respective point amount × quantity; cost in ₹ = selected round-trip point cost × quantity. No compounding, margin or account-return claim. The user-selected size is applied uniformly across all dates, not as a historical contract specification.
  4. Limitations: historical OHLC does not establish tick order, executable liquidity or achievable close fills. Index prices and the trading instrument may differ. This is one historical sample, with no holdout validation or parameter optimization. Repeated input experiments introduce selection bias.

Strategy rules · IST

PDH / PDL only · Custom points & time

No ORB range or EMA filter. PDH/PDL are the last completed trading-session high/low. Starting strictly after the selected Entry after time (default 09:30), a selected 1/3/5-minute completed close strictly above PDH gives LONG; strictly below PDL gives SHORT. Entry is the qualifying close, not the PDH/PDL level. LONG SL = entry − selected SL points, target = entry + selected target points. SHORT SL = entry + selected SL points, target = entry − selected target points. Defaults are 50 SL / 100 target. With Entry after = 09:30, the first eligible 1-minute candle is 09:31 (confirmed 09:32); 3-minute is 09:33–09:35 (confirmed 09:36); 5-minute is 09:35–09:39 (confirmed 09:40). Candles align to the 09:15 session and must start strictly after the selected Entry after time. For 10:00, the first eligible 1-minute candle is 10:01, confirmed at 10:02. These are NIFTY spot points, not option premiums. One entry by default. If Two entries is enabled, only a first SL allows one later opposite PDH/PDL breakout; no immediate Pine reversal and no ORB check. Exits use subsequent 1-minute candles, opening-gap priority and SL-first on ambiguous candles. Time exit is the 15:10 candle (known at 15:11). Missing required data from the minute after the selected entry time through 15:10 or previous-session levels skips the day. Option legs, premiums, lots and fill rules remain configurable.

Plain ORB · No PDH/PDL or EMA

Default range 09:20–09:30 inclusive, editable. After the range completes, enter on the selected 1/3/5-minute candle close strictly above ORB High (LONG) or below ORB Low (SHORT). No 10 AM wait; no PDH/PDL or EMA filter. Risk = ORB width × Stop setting (default 1). Stop is entry minus risk for LONG, entry plus risk for SHORT. Target = selected R × risk (default 2). One entry by default. If Two entries is selected, first SL permits one later opposite ORB breakout using the selected candle timeframe; it does not use the Pine immediate reversal. Exit checks use 1-minute candles after entry, gap priority and SL-first for ambiguous bars. Time exit uses the 15:10 bar, confirmed at 15:11. First/second lots and option fill rules remain configurable.

Entry candle confirmation · 1 / 3 / 5 minutes

Default 1 minute. The selected timeframe applies to breakout entries in every strategy. Candles align to the 09:15 IST session start and must begin after the ORB ends and at/after the strategy entry start. For From 10 AM modes: 1-minute 10:00 candle confirms at 10:01; 3-minute 10:00–10:02 confirms at 10:03; 5-minute 10:00–10:04 confirms at 10:05. Only the completed candle close qualifies; an earlier wick or intermediate minute close does not. ORB still uses the selected inclusive 1-minute range. Stops, targets and exits still use 1-minute candles. Pine immediate second-entry SL reversal keeps its original rule and does not wait for a 3/5-minute close. Options use snapshots at/after confirmation. The exported signal minute is the last 1-minute bar of the confirmation candle.

Pine ORB + PDH/PDL · From 10 AM · SL Reverse

Matches the supplied indicator: default ORB 09:20–09:30 inclusive; first entry starts with the 10:00 candle (known at 10:01), or the next candle after ORB if later, as requested. First LONG close must be strictly above ORB High and PDH; first SHORT close strictly below ORB Low and PDL. No EMA. SL distance is one ORB width, rounded to the NIFTY 0.05 tick; target uses actual rounded stop risk × selected R (default 2). One entry is the script default. Select Two entries to enable exactly one immediate opposite reversal after first Stop/Stop gap, at that stop price or gap open; no second breakout or PDH/PDL check. The reversal signal is only known at the SL candle close. Exit checks start on the following candle, never on the reversal candle itself. Opening stop gap wins, then opening target gap; otherwise SL wins if both touch. Target gaps use target price. Default time exit is the candle closing at 15:10 (09:20–09:30 range remains editable). No reversal at/after time exit; no reversal after target/time exit; no third trade. First/second lots use the existing per-leg inputs. Options still fill at the first eligible snapshot after signal confirmation, NOT at the historical spot SL price. A skipped first option basket suppresses its second basket. Missing required minute data is excluded and audited.

ORB 09:20–09:30 · Boundary SL · 1:2 (new)

Build the range using all eleven 1-minute candles labelled 09:20 through 09:30. Starting with the 09:31 candle, the first completed close strictly above ORB High gives LONG; strictly below ORB Low gives SHORT. A wick touch or equal close does not qualify. No PDH/PDL or EMA filter. LONG: SL = ORB Low, risk = entry close − ORB Low, target = entry close + 2 × risk. SHORT: SL = ORB High, risk = ORB High − entry close, target = entry close − 2 × risk. Range and 1:2 target are fixed for this strategy. Selecting it defaults to one entry per day.

ORB 09:20–09:30 + PDH/PDL · Boundary SL · 1:2

Separate filtered version of Boundary SL ORB. Use all eleven candles labelled 09:20–09:30; first eligible entry candle is 09:31. LONG requires a completed 1-minute close strictly above BOTH ORB High and previous trading-day High (PDH). SHORT requires a close strictly below BOTH ORB Low and previous trading-day Low (PDL). Missing PDH/PDL skips the day. No EMA filter. LONG SL = ORB Low; SHORT SL = ORB High. Target is 2 times the entry-to-stop risk. Selecting this variant defaults to one entry per day. If two entries is enabled, the existing opposite-ORB-only re-entry rule after first SL still applies without PDH/PDL.

PDH / PDL + ORB · After range (no 10 AM wait)

Build the selected ORB range, inclusive of the last labelled candle. Start checking the next 1-minute candle immediately, without waiting for 10:00. For a 09:20–09:30 range, the first eligible candle is 09:31 (known at 09:32). LONG requires a completed close strictly above BOTH ORB High and PDH; SHORT strictly below BOTH ORB Low and PDL. Wick touches and equal closes do not qualify. No EMA filter. Risk = ORB width × Stop setting, SL = entry minus risk for LONG / entry plus risk for SHORT, target = selected R × risk from entry. This uses the original risk model, not boundary SL. Missing previous-day levels skip the day. Daily entry limits and optional opposite-ORB-only reentry follow the shared rules below.

PDH / PDL + ORB · From 10 AM

Use the selected ORB interval. First entry is at or after 10:00 (or the first bar after ORB, if later). LONG close must exceed both ORB High and previous trading-day High. SHORT close must be below both ORB Low and previous trading-day Low. Risk = ORB width × Stop setting. SL = entry − risk for LONG, entry + risk for SHORT. Target uses the selected R multiple.

EMA20 bias + ORB

Uses previous-day close versus previous-day EMA20, not an intraday EMA9. Above EMA gives LONG bias; below gives SHORT bias. The EMA distance filter must pass. From 10:00 or after ORB, enter when a close breaks ORB in the bias direction. Risk = ORB width × Stop setting; target uses the selected R multiple.

Original strategy exits, daily entries and shared option fills

Exit checks start on the bar after entry. Stop gaps use the bar open. Target gaps use the target price. If stop and target both touch with no decisive opening gap, stop wins. Unclosed positions exit on the 15:10 spot candle, known at 15:11. One entry means no re-entry even after SL. If Two entries is selected, only a first Stop/Stop gap permits one later opposite ORB signal; PDH/PDL and EMA filters do not apply to that second signal. First and second lots are set per leg; the first basket must execute, and baskets cannot overlap. Options fill at the first available snapshot after the completed signal/exit candle within Maximum fill delay. Spot SL and target levels are not option-premium SL/target levels. Missing required minute bars or contract quotes are audited.
