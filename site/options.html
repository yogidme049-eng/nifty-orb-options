02 Signal & trading days

Maximum two baskets per day when enabled: after the first spot SL/stop gap, wait for an opposite-direction close beyond the ORB on a later bar. No re-entry after target/time exit; no third entry. PDH/PDL and EMA filters apply to the first entry only. After its stop, the second entry needs only an opposite ORB breakout. First and second lots are set per leg. A skipped first basket cannot trigger a second basket. The new Boundary SL strategy starts after 09:30 and uses the opposite range boundary as SL; other modes retain their original risk rules. All modes use the 15:10 exit bar. Full strategy rules are listed below. Weekday filters affect entries, not previous-day levels. Embedded spot history ends 29 Sep 2026. September 30 option data is synced but cannot generate a spot signal; it appears in the audit.

Data & execution audit

How fills are synchronized

  1. One-minute spot candles generate signals. Boundary SL mode can first signal on the 09:31 candle (known at 09:32); original modes start at 10:00 or after ORB. The bar labelled 10:00 is known at 10:01. Options use the first observed snapshot at or after 10:01, within the chosen maximum delay. For 5-minute data this is usually 10:05. Set delay to 0 for exact-minute matching.
  2. The same rule applies after the completed spot exit bar. No interpolation or stale-price forward filling. If the spot trade exits before the delayed option entry, the basket is skipped. The spot strategy timeline is retained; delayed option fills can produce different losses from the spot stop.
  3. All legs enter together, lock their expiry and strike, and exit together. Missing required contract prices exclude the entire basket. Nearest expiry means nearest available in the snapshot, not a verified exchange calendar.
  4. Snapshot LTPs are research fill proxies, not bid/ask execution prices. Premium P&L uses signed quantity, adverse slippage and per-lot fees. Fixed lot size applies to every year; no historical lot-size schedule is inferred. Margin, account returns and intratrade drawdown are not modeled.
  5. Zero, blank and conflicting prices are excluded. Missing source dates and missing spot sessions remain visible in the audit. Option coverage is limited to the supplied files, not the full ten-year spot history. All times are IST.

Strategy rules · IST

ORB 09:20–09:30 · Boundary SL · 1:2 (new)

Build the range using all eleven 1-minute candles labelled 09:20 through 09:30. Starting with the 09:31 candle, the first completed close strictly above ORB High gives LONG; strictly below ORB Low gives SHORT. A wick touch or equal close does not qualify. No PDH/PDL or EMA filter. LONG: SL = ORB Low, risk = entry close − ORB Low, target = entry close + 2 × risk. SHORT: SL = ORB High, risk = ORB High − entry close, target = entry close − 2 × risk. Range and 1:2 target are fixed for this strategy. Selecting it defaults to one entry per day.

PDH / PDL + ORB

Use the selected ORB interval. First entry is at or after 10:00 (or the first bar after ORB, if later). LONG close must exceed both ORB High and previous trading-day High. SHORT close must be below both ORB Low and previous trading-day Low. Risk = ORB width × Stop setting. SL = entry − risk for LONG, entry + risk for SHORT. Target uses the selected R multiple.

EMA20 bias + ORB

Uses previous-day close versus previous-day EMA20, not an intraday EMA9. Above EMA gives LONG bias; below gives SHORT bias. The EMA distance filter must pass. From 10:00 or after ORB, enter when a close breaks ORB in the bias direction. Risk = ORB width × Stop setting; target uses the selected R multiple.

Shared exits, daily entries and option fills

Exit checks start on the bar after entry. Stop gaps use the bar open. Target gaps use the target price. If stop and target both touch with no decisive opening gap, stop wins. Unclosed positions exit on the 15:10 spot candle, known at 15:11. One entry means no re-entry even after SL. If Two entries is selected, only a first Stop/Stop gap permits one later opposite ORB signal; PDH/PDL and EMA filters do not apply to that second signal. First and second lots are set per leg; the first basket must execute, and baskets cannot overlap. Options fill at the first available snapshot after the completed signal/exit candle within Maximum fill delay. Spot SL and target levels are not option-premium SL/target levels. Missing required minute bars or contract quotes are audited.
