02 Signal & trading days

Maximum two baskets per day when enabled: after the first spot SL/stop gap, wait for an opposite-direction close beyond the ORB on a later bar. No re-entry after target/time exit; no third entry. PDH/PDL and EMA filters apply to the first entry only. After its stop, the second entry needs only an opposite ORB breakout. First and second lots are set per leg. A skipped first basket cannot trigger a second basket. Pure ORB follows the first close above/below the range after 10:00 (or after the range ends, if later), without previous-day or EMA filters. All modes retain the original index stop, target and 15:10 exit bar. Weekday filters affect entries, not previous-day levels. Embedded spot history ends 29 Sep 2026. September 30 option data is synced but cannot generate a spot signal; it appears in the audit.

03 Build your option basket

Choose a preset to preview its legs. Applying replaces the current basket; all legs remain editable.

Default ORB spread: 4 × ₹50 = 200 points. Upside sells ATM PE and buys PE 200 lower; downside sells ATM CE and buys CE 200 higher. Both legs have the same expiry and lots. Presets share the selected expiry and the existing ORB entry/exit signals. The ORB signal credit spread switches PE/CE automatically. Other fixed bullish/bearish presets do not reverse on the opposite signal. Preset lots apply to the first entry; set second-entry lots separately below. These are intraday tests, not expiry-payoff simulations.

Current basket: Custom

Follow signal: CE on LONG, PE on SHORT. Opposite: PE on LONG, CE on SHORT. Use Buy + Follow for directional buying; Sell + Opposite for directional selling. ATM is rounded from the option snapshot’s spot (or signal spot if unavailable); Signed offsets move strikes up (+) or down (−). OTM mode moves PE strikes lower and CE strikes higher automatically. Data & execution audit

How fills are synchronized

  1. One-minute spot candles generate Pure ORB, EMA20 or PDH/PDL ORB signals. The bar labelled 10:00 is known at 10:01. Options use the first observed snapshot at or after 10:01, within the chosen maximum delay. For 5-minute data this is usually 10:05. Set delay to 0 for exact-minute matching.
  2. The same rule applies after the completed spot exit bar. No interpolation or stale-price forward filling. If the spot trade exits before the delayed option entry, the basket is skipped. The spot strategy timeline is retained; delayed option fills can produce different losses from the spot stop.
  3. All legs enter together, lock their expiry and strike, and exit together. Missing required contract prices exclude the entire basket. Nearest expiry means nearest available in the snapshot, not a verified exchange calendar.
  4. Snapshot LTPs are research fill proxies, not bid/ask execution prices. Premium P&L uses signed quantity, adverse slippage and per-lot fees. Fixed lot size applies to every year; no historical lot-size schedule is inferred. Margin, account returns and intratrade drawdown are not modeled.
  5. Zero, blank and conflicting prices are excluded. Missing source dates and missing spot sessions remain visible in the audit. Option coverage is limited to the supplied files, not the full ten-year spot history. All times are IST.
